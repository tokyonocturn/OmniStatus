# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want contributing to OmniStatus to be a straightforward, safe experience for anyone, regardless of experience level, background, identity, or employer. We pledge to make participation harassment free.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct and technical about code, and considerate about people.
- Assume good faith and ask before assuming intent.
- Accept review feedback on its merits.
- Credit other people's work.
- Respect privacy. Do not post another person's logs, tokens, browsing data, or personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory comments about identity, background, or experience.
- Sexualized language or attention in project spaces.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues, or pull requests.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a problem.

## Scope

This applies in repository spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews), in project chat spaces, and when representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report incidents to the maintainers through the contact listed on the repository profile, or by using GitHub's reporting tools. Reports are handled privately. Maintainers will acknowledge receipt and explain the next step.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take any action they consider proportionate:

1. A private or public clarification and a request to change behavior.
2. A warning with a temporary restriction on interaction.
3. Temporary removal from project spaces.
4. Permanent removal from project spaces and rejection of contributions.

Decisions are made by the maintainers, documented privately, and may be appealed once by replying to the enforcement message.
